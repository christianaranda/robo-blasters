TUTORIAL

Welcome to Robo Blasters!

You're a skilled marksman tasked with eliminating enemy robots in an arena. This tutorial will teach you everything you need to know.

Use the arrow buttons below to navigate through the tutorial.

Movement Controls

WASD Keys - Move your character:

  • W - Move forward
  • S - Move backward
  • A - Strafe left
  • D - Strafe right

Space - Jump to reach higher platforms and avoid obstacles

Mouse - Move your mouse to look around and aim

Combat Basics

Left Click - Fire your weapon at enemies

R Key - Reload when you run out of ammo

1-5 Keys - Switch between different weapons:

  • 1 - Pistol (balanced)
  • 2 - Rifle (accurate, high damage)
  • 3 - Shotgun (close range, high damage)
  • 4 - SMG (rapid fire)
  • 5 - Sniper (long range, very high damage)

Weapon Attachments

T Key - Open the weapon attachments menu

You can equip up to 2 attachments per weapon to enhance their capabilities:

  • Extended Magazine - More ammo capacity
  • Rapid Fire - Faster firing rate
  • Damage Boost - Increased damage
  • Scope - Better accuracy and zoom
  • Silencer - Reduced recoil

Experiment with different combinations to find your preferred playstyle!

Gameplay Tips

Objective: Defeat all enemy robots in the arena to win!

Health & Ammo: Keep an eye on your health (top-left) and ammo count. If health reaches zero, it's game over!

Power-ups: Collect power-ups scattered around the arena for temporary advantages like health regeneration or damage boosts

Movement: Keep moving! Standing still makes you an easy target. Use cover and jump on platforms to avoid enemy fire

Scoring: Earn points by destroying enemies. Try to beat your high score!

Other Controls

P Key - Pause the game to access settings, view leaderboard, or take a break

H Key - Open this tutorial anytime during gameplay

Mini-map: Check the top-right corner for enemy positions and power-up locations

Ready to Play!

You're now ready to take on the enemy robots!

Difficulty Levels:

  • Easy - 3 robots (good for learning)
  • Medium - 5 robots (balanced challenge)
  • Hard - 8 robots (for experienced players)

Select your difficulty and click "Start Game" to begin your mission!

Good luck, and remember: stay mobile, aim true, and watch your ammo!
